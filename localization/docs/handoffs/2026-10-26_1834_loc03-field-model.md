## LOC-03 `td_field_model`: what exists

- `td_field_model/field.py`:
  - `FieldModel.builtin("hsl_{s,m,l}_2026")` and `.from_json(venue.json)`.
  - Line centerlines as `Segment` and `Arc`.
  - Typed landmarks: L/T/X crossings, center mark, penalty marks, goalposts.
  - `distance()`, `sample_lines()`, `in_bounds()`, `likelihood_field()` → `LikelihoodField` (bilinear `lookup`,
    `save`/`load` as `.npz`).
  - `mirror_pose()`, `mirror_points()`.
- **Frame:** origin at the center mark, +x toward the opponent goal; own goal at −x.
- `fields/*.json` vendored from HSL-Rules `rules-2026-v1.1.1`, with sha256 in `fields/SOURCE.md`.
- `test/test_field.py`: 46 tests pass. Ruff check and format are clean with `td_perception/ruff.toml`.

**Findings to tell the team:**
1. **L-Field JSON discrepancy:** `length` and `width` (21.95 / 13.95) imply 22.07 × 14.07 m outside, not the rule
   table's 22 × 14. All other L values use the 0.12 m line width correctly. The discrepancy is pinned by a test and
   documented in `SOURCE.md`. It could be reported upstream, but only by a human, since it's a GitHub action.
2. Goal posts are placed on the goal-line centerline. The rules don't say exactly; the error is at most half a line width.
3. The likelihood-field build for L at 2 cm takes about 2.5 s (target was 2 s). Cache it with `save()`/`load()`.
4. **Fixed bug:** the grid could stop one cell short of the far border when the span isn't a whole number of cells.
