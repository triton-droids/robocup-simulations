## What was added (`localization_work/td_localization/`)

### `td_localization/eval.py` (LOC-07)

Library and CLI that follows the conventions of td_perception's `evaluation_perception/motion_eval.py`:
- Nearest estimate within a tolerance, no interpolation.
- Missing estimates count as failures.
- Uncomputable metrics are N/A, never zero.

**CSV contracts:**
- Ground truth: `timestamp_s,x_m,y_m,theta_rad[,event]`, with events `initial`, `fall`, `kidnap`, `penalty_reentry`.
- Estimates: `timestamp_s,status,x_m,y_m,theta_rad[,side_confidence][,published_s]`, with status `localized`,
  `uncertain`, `initializing` or `lost`.

**Metrics:**
- accuracy within threshold (0.5 m and 5°), availability;
- position p50/p95/max/RMSE, heading p50/p95;
- wrong-half fraction, episodes and longest episode (heading-aware mirror test);
- **confidently-wrong samples** (wrong half with side confidence ≥ 0.9);
- side confidence when right vs wrong;
- latency p50/p95;
- **convergence time after each event**;
- PASS/FAIL checks against roadmap §3.3.

**Outputs:** `report.md`, `results.json`, `samples_<run>.csv`. Compares several runs side by side.

**CLI:**
- `python -m td_localization.eval evaluate --gt gt.csv --est a.csv [--est b.csv] --out DIR`
- `python -m td_localization.eval suite --out DIR [--noise harsh] [--seed N] [--only name]`
- Also a `loc_eval` console entry point in `setup.py`.

### `td_localization/suite.py`

Reusable simulation runner, `simulate()`: filter + side belief + ball + teammates → `GroundTruth` / `EstimateTrack`.
- Status mapping: lost → `initializing`; degraded or weight < 0.8 → `uncertain`; otherwise `localized`.
- `published_s` = stamp + this machine's compute time.

Seven standard scenarios: `tracking`, `global_sideline`, `penalty_reentry`, `falls`, `kidnap_same_half`,
`kidnap_cross_team`, `kidnap_cross_alone`.

**Kidnaps are now defined relative to the pre-kidnap pose:**
- `kidnap_same_half`: move 1.5 m and turn 0.6 rad, so continuity holds.
- `kidnap_to_mirror`: go to the mirror of the current pose plus a small offset. This is truly adversarial:
  continuity points to the wrong half.

### Side-belief fixes found by the suite (`side.py`)
1. **Jump detection is now position-based** (> 1.5 m), plus heading changes > 90°. Before, a 60° fall turn counted
   as a "jump" and dropped confidence after every fall (0.82 → now 0.997).
2. **2 s grace period after lost mode ends.** Before, the estimate settling after a global search counted as a jump
   (confidence 0.73 → now 0.994).

## Findings to act on
1. **Filter weakness (LOC-05), exposed by the suite.**
   - Same-half kidnap, seed 1: pushed toward the touchline, the filter locked 0.7–1 m off for about 10 s while
     reporting `localized` (aliasing along the touchline).
   - Recovery didn't trigger because match quality stayed "OK".
   - Candidate fixes:
     - A slow drift detector (persistent moderate mismatch) that injects proposals.
     - Weighting landmarks higher.
     - The "pose inconsistent" signal from teammate reports that match neither directly nor mirrored (LOC-06
       follow-up).
   - Reproduce: `... eval suite --out reports/x --only kidnap_same_half --seed 1`.
2. **"Confidently wrong" samples in cross-field kidnaps:** 7–25 samples, about 0.25–0.8 s. That's the lag between
   the kidnap and the estimate jumping, which is inherent. It's now measured, so improvements can be tracked.
3. Earlier LOC-05/06 kidnap tests used fixed targets, so whether they were "same half" or "cross" depended on the
   seed. They pass, but should be converted to `kidnap_same_half()` / `kidnap_to_mirror()` for clarity (small
   follow-up).
