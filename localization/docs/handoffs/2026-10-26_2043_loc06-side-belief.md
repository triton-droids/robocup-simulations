## What was added (`localization_work/td_localization/`)

### `td_localization/side.py`

`SideBelief(pf, SideConfig)` holds `logodds` (filter's half correct vs mirrored).
- **Outputs:** `confidence` = sigmoid(logodds); `avoid_shooting` (confidence < 0.9); `flips`; `events` log.
- **Game events:**
  - `on_initial(t, known_poses | None)` → `pf.init_poses` or `pf.init_uniform(own_half)`; logodds = 6.
  - `on_penalty_reentry(t, candidates)` → `pf.init_poses`; logodds = 6.
- **Evidence:**
  - `on_my_ball(t, ball_xy_field, sigma)`: only stored while my pose is trustworthy.
  - `on_team_report(TeamReport)`: the llr = (d_mirror² − d_direct²)/(2σ²), clipped to ±2.
    - Gated: neither direct nor mirrored within 3σ → 0.
    - Weighted by the teammate's confidence (ignored below 0.6). Goalkeeper ×2.
  - `on_opponents_at_set(t, xy_list)`: ±0.5 per opponent beyond ±0.5 m.
- **`step(t, estimate)` once per cycle:**
  - Decays logodds (τ 6 s) while the filter is lost or degraded.
  - **Caps logodds at 1.0 when the estimate jumps > 1.5 m** (pose distance, heading weighted).
  - Flips (`pf.mirror()`) after logodds < −4 for 1 s. The new side starts at +3, and there's a 3 s refractory
    period.
- `TeamReport(t, robot_id, ball_xy, ball_sigma, side_confidence, is_goalkeeper)` is the per-message payload LOC-21
  needs.

## Results (simulation, M-Field)

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Cross-field kidnap + 3 teammates (6 seeds, 3 targets) | correct side within ≤ 4.2 s, at most 1 flip, final error ≤ 0.06 m |
| Cross-field kidnap, no teammates | stays mirrored (**known limit**), but confidence drops to about 0.7, so `avoid_shooting` = True |
| Normal play (10 seeds, half harsh noise, falls, one unsure flipped teammate) | **0 flips**; 4 transient wrong-side frames out of about 15,000, from the filter itself |
| One *confidently* wrong teammate + 2 correct | 0 flips, confidence stays > 0.98 |
| Penalty re-entry after an unresolved kidnap | correct side again within 2 s |

## Bugs found and fixed this run
1. A kidnap left confidence at 0.98 while on the wrong side. Fix: jump detection caps confidence.
2. Ball reports compared against a wrong (non-mirror) pose gave random evidence and caused flip-flopping (3 flips).
   Fix: a 3σ match gate, plus a moderate post-flip confidence and the refractory period.

## Limits and notes
- **A majority of confidently wrong teammates will flip us wrongly.** This is by design: we trust the team. The
  goalkeeper weighting helps.
- **When neither direct nor mirrored balls match** (my pose is wrong but not mirrored), the side belief ignores
  the report.
  - A useful follow-up: count those mismatches as a "pose inconsistent" signal so LOC-20 can trigger filter
    relocalization. Not implemented.
- The ball model is a stand-in. Real ball state will come from the `td_ball_tracker` BallState (in
  base_footprint), mapped through the filter pose.
- Not handled yet: half-time (handlers re-place robots → `on_initial`), `GameController` parsing (LOC-08), message
  packing and budget (LOC-21).
