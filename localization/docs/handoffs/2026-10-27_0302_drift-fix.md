## Problem (found by the LOC-07 suite)

After a push toward the touchline, the cloud collapsed about 1 m off and crept back at about 0.1 m/s for about
10 s, while reporting `localized`.
- The true pose scored far better on every frame (log-likelihood −10 to −60 vs −150 to −300).
- The quality-drop trigger is relative to a slowly adapting baseline, so it rarely fired.
- Global proposals almost never land near a pose only about 1 m away.

## Fix: local drift test (`td_localization/pf.py`)

`ParticleFilter._drift_test()` runs on each informative frame when the filter isn't lost.
1. Score 200 candidates around the best cluster on 40 of the frame's points. Half are near (σ 0.6 m, 0.25 rad) and
   half are wide (σ 1.5 m, 0.6 rad).
2. Per-point gap = (best candidate − estimate) / points.
3. Fire when the gap is > 0.5 on ≥ 3 of the last 4 informative frames, then **keep firing every frame while it
   persists**. Each firing replaces 5% of the particles with the top candidates.
- New outputs: `pf.drift` and `UpdateStats.drift`. In `suite.py` the status is `uncertain` while drift is detected.
- New config: `drift_candidates`, `drift_sigma_xy/theta`, `drift_wide_sigma_xy/theta`, `drift_margin_per_point`,
  `drift_window`, `drift_votes`, `drift_inject_fraction`.
- **Side-safe:** candidates are near the estimate, so they can't move the robot to the other half.
