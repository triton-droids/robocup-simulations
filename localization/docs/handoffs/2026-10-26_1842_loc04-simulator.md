## What was added (`localization_work/td_field_model/`)

### `td_field_model/sim.py`

`Simulator(field, CameraModel, NoiseModel, seed).render(pose, head_yaw, head_pitch, obstacles, t)` → `Observations`.
- **Method:**
  1. Project field points to D435 pixels from the **true** camera pose (848×480, fx = fy = 615, height 1.2 m).
  2. Add pixel noise.
  3. Project back to the floor with the **believed** pose (pitch bias + per-frame pitch and yaw jitter).

  Range error that grows with distance squared comes out of the geometry.
- **`Observations`** (robot frame, base_footprint):
  - `line_points` (N, 2), `line_cov` (N, 2, 2), `line_range_ok` (False beyond `bearing_only_beyond`, 4 m).
  - `landmarks: list[ObservedLandmark]` (kind, xy, cov, range_ok, truth_name).
  - Test-only fields: `line_is_false`, `true_pose`.
- **`NoiseModel` knobs:**
  - `pixel_sigma`, `pitch_bias`, `pitch_jitter`, `yaw_jitter`;
  - `point_dropout`, `frame_dropout`, `false_positives` (Poisson per frame);
  - `crossing_confusion` (L/T/X mixed up);
  - `max_line_points` (default 200; `None` = no cap).
  - `NoiseModel.zero()` gives exact, complete output.
- **`Obstacle(x, y, radius)`:** a tall cylinder that hides the floor behind it (2-D sight-line test).
- **Helpers:** `world_to_robot`, `robot_to_world`, `CameraModel.pose_in_base(yaw, pitch)`.

### `td_field_model/scenarios.py`

`ScenarioBuilder(field, seed, dt=1/30, OdometryNoise, HeadMotion)` is chainable:
- `.start(pose, known_poses)`, `.walk(s, speed)`, `.turn(rad)`, `.stand(s)`;
- `.fall()`: heading changes, odometry reports nothing with a large covariance;
- `.kidnap(pose)`;
- `.penalty_reentry("left"|"right")`: both rule-based candidates go in `known_poses`.

`.steps` is a list of `Step(t, true_pose, odom (dx, dy, dθ robot frame), odom_cov, head_yaw, head_pitch, event,
known_poses)`.

Pose helpers: `penalty_reentry_poses`, `kickoff_poses`, `sideline_start_poses`, `compose`, `relative_motion`.

### Tests

`test/test_sim.py` (20) and `test/test_scenarios.py` (12) are new. The package total is **78 passing**; ruff check and
format are clean.

Key checks:
- Noise-free points and landmarks lie exactly on the field.
- Every point is inside the image and within range.
- **What the robot sees from a pose fits the mirrored pose perfectly** (the symmetry premise for LOC-06).
- Error grows with distance; covariance consistency (mean NEES 1.2–3.0); determinism.
- Occlusion; noise-free odometry integrates back to the truth.

**Speed:** 120 s of 30 Hz frames (3,603) render in 5.7 s, 1.6 ms per frame, with about 180 line points and 4
landmarks per frame. That meets the LOC-04 "LOC-05/06 tests under 30 s" goal.

## Behavior to know
- Line points are a random subset (up to 200) of visible points sampled every 3 cm along lines. The detector's real
  sampling pattern is unknown, so revisit this in LOC-17.
- False positives are random pixels in the lower image half projected to the floor. There's no "adjacent field
  lines" model yet.
- Goalposts are observed at their base point on the goal line, like other landmarks. Post height and occlusion by
  the crossbar aren't modeled.
- The walker steers back 0.3 m plus its turning radius inside the lines, so it can stray about 0.3 m outside.
