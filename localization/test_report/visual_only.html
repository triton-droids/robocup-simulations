Visual-only localization test

cuVSLAM (model) + camera + head angles from encoders/IMU · generated 2026-10-29 17:24 by test_report/visual_only.py · 3 seeds × 5 variants × 30 s · 7.9 min

What is real and what is modelled. The localization code (particle filter, side belief), the image renderer and the line detector are the real bench code. cuVSLAM is a model (SimVisualOdometry): it can't run on this machine, which has no NVIDIA GPU and no Isaac ROS. The model is stereo-inertial VO with a 3% scale error, 5% translation noise, 1.5°/min gyro bias and head-motion subtraction using the believed head angles. Tracking drops while the head pans faster than 1.2 rad/s and in random 1 s episodes (2% per second). The head angles are also a model (ImuHead). Real cuVSLAM output plugs in through LiveVisualOdometry.push_pose(). Targets are the roadmap §3.3 ones.
